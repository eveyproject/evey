# On every screen

One server, one library, the same resume points everywhere. Every app talks to
your own server: you type its address once, sign in, pick a profile, and you
are home.

## In a browser

The web app is served by the server itself, at the address you open after the
install: nothing to install on the computer. It carries the whole experience -
films, series, music, photos, Watch Together - plus the administration panel
for administrators. On a computer, the player answers the keyboard (press `?`
to see the shortcuts).

## On an Android phone or tablet

The Android app adds what a phone is good for:

- **downloads** for offline viewing, with a smaller copy prepared by the
  server when the original would not fit;
- **casting** to a Chromecast from the player;
- **scanning a Quick Connect code** to sign a television in without typing a
  password with a remote;
- **push notifications** when new films, episodes or music arrive, at the
  level you choose for each, if the server is set up for them.

## On an Android TV

The TV app is designed for the remote: big posters, rows you walk with the
arrows, a player whose controls stay out of the way. Signing in does not
require typing a password: the TV shows a **Quick Connect** code (and a QR
code), you approve it from a phone or a computer where you are already signed
in, and the TV is in.

## On a Chromecast

From the web player in Chrome, or from the Android app, the **Cast** button
sends the film to a Chromecast or a TV with Chromecast built in. The server
drives it directly with its own receiver, so what plays on the TV is chosen for
the TV - its codecs, its screen, its connection - not for the phone that sent
it.

## From one Evey app to another

**Play on** sends what you are watching to another Evey app that is open on
the same account - the TV app in the living room, the phone on the desk - and
it starts there. The button lists the devices that are online right now.

## Which one plays best?

The server checks what each device can decode and adapts, so the same film may
be sent untouched to the TV and converted for an old phone. You can see the
decision, and its reasons, in the player's **Stream info** panel.
